- **Several accounts in one browser.** Stay signed in to up to six accounts and switch from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, or from Settings on a phone.
- **People on this server.** The first account is the admin. Admins add people, reset passwords, make other admins, remove accounts and open or close sign-ups from Settings. They see names and run counts, never anyone's runs.
- **Delete your own account** from Settings.
- **Fuller dashboard.** Six summary tiles, a 12-month calendar beside your pace goal, recent runs beside personal records, and race predictions as a full-width strip. Paired cards end level.
- **New sidebar.** Your name and an account menu at the bottom, this week's distance as daily bars, and a run count beside Runs.
- **Zoom and pan** the trend charts with the buttons, dragging, Ctrl + scroll or double-click, plus a timeline strip of your whole history.

Upgrading: in your spur folder run `git pull` then `docker compose up -d --build`. Your 1.0.0 database is upgraded on start, and its oldest account becomes the admin.
